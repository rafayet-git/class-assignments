###### A major topic in linera algebra is solving system of linear equations

2x2 linear system: 2 equations and 2 variables. We solve by using the additive method or subtractive method

Example:

$$
-4x+4y=8 \\ 4x + 2y = 10 \\ Solution: (1,3)
$$

Some have no solution if x or y are different

$$
x-y=3 \rightarrow y = x-3 \\ -2x+3y-4 \rightarrow y = x-2
$$

2 equations represent the same line, they are multiples of each other. Infinitenty many solutions

$$
x-y=2 \\ 2x-2y=4 \\ 0 = 0
$$

We represent solutions by forming a parameterization $x=y+2$ . y is called a "free variable", as y can be chosen freely but x is determined

Replace y with another letter such as s, called a parameter. The solution is the form $(s+2, s)$ 

<u>Definition</u>: The set of solutions to a linear system is called the solution set.

<u>Definition</u>: A linear system is consistent if it has at least 1 solution. It is inconsistent if it has no solutions

<u>Definition</u>: Two linear systems with the same variables are equivalent if they have the same solution set

How to obtain equivalent systems:

1. Interchange two equations

2. Multiply an equation by a nonzero number

3. Replace equation by the sum of itself and a multiple fo other equation

$$
-x + y = 2 \\ 4x+2y=10 \rightarrow  6y = 18 \rightarrow  y=3\\ -x=-1 \rightarrow x=1
$$

### Matrices

A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers

For a linear system, we can associate the matrix consisting of the coefficents of variables called the coefficient matrix

$$
3x_1 - 2x_2 +4x_3 = 5\\ -2x_1+5x_2-6x_3=2\\2x_2+7x_3=-1 \\
x_1 \space x_2 \space x_3 \\ EQs
\begin{bmatrix}
3 & -2 & 4 \\
-2 & 5 & -6 \\
0 & 2 & 7
\end{bmatrix}
$$

We can add on the 3x1 matrix consisting of the constants of the RHS called the augment matrix

$$
\left[

  3&-2&4&5\\
