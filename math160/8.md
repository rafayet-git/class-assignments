Definition: Row equivalence - b If row equivalent to A is B can be obtained from A by a sequence elementary row operations.

A is nonsingular when Ax=b has a unique solution and also when A is row equivalent to I.

Provisional theorem: Let A be an nxn matrix, The following are equivalent: 

1. A is nonsingular

2. Ax=b has a unique soln

3. A is row equivalet to I.
