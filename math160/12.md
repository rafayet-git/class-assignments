Prove all axioms to prove vector space

<u>Application</u> springs: If a mass m is placed at the end of a spring, and if the mass is pulled downwards and released, the mass-spring system will begin to oscillate. The displacement y of the mass from its resting position is given by a function of the form $y(E) = C_1cos(wt)+C_2sin(wt)$, where w is a constant that depends on the spring + the mass the set of all such functions form a vector space.
